Tuning tips

  • Tune from the thickest string (low E) to the thinnest.
  • Always tune up to the note: if you go past it, drop below and come back up.
  • Electric guitars are quiet unplugged. Hold your phone near the guitar body and pluck firmly, or use the amp's volume at a low level.
  • New strings stretch and drift. Re-check the tuning every time you sit down.
